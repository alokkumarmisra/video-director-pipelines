# Lyric Video Preset

## Purpose
Create elegant visuals where lyrics are the star and always readable.

## Visual Style
- Large kinetic typography with generous safe margins
- Lyrics legible on every frame: high contrast, no busy overlap
- Atmospheric but quiet backgrounds: gradients, particles, slow vistas
- Word-by-word emphasis synced to imagined phrasing
- Restrained palette that lets text lead
- Clean modern type with consistent font personality

## Storytelling
Let the words carry meaning; visuals illustrate gently without competing. Each line gets its visual breath — never rush the reader.

## Characters
If a singer appears, keep them soft-focus or secondary. Typography consistency matters more than character detail here.

## Camera
Use calm readable moves: slow drifts, gentle zooms, static holds on key lines. Motion never blurs the text.

## Lighting
Use soft even illumination on text zones with moody-but-dim backgrounds. Readability beats drama every time.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Tiny fonts, text over cluttered bright areas, misspelled words, competing focal action, and strobing effects that hurt reading.
