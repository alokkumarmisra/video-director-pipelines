# Silent Movie Preset

## Purpose
Create expressive dialogue-free cinema in the spirit of the silent era.

## Visual Style
- Expressive black-and-white or sepia-toned imagery (or selective color)
- Theatrical yet sincere performances readable without words
- Period staging: vintage streets, parlors, factories, rooftops
- Iris shots, vignettes, and title-card-like pauses between beats
- Visible film texture: gentle grain, soft flicker, rounded frame feel
- Props as language: flowers, letters, hats, canes tell the story

## Storytelling
Play emotion large and sincerely: longing looks, comic chases, tearful reunions. Intertitle moments — hold a beat so the feeling lands like text on screen.

## Characters
Use distinct silent-era silhouettes: fixed costumes, hats, and makeup. Faces must act — every feeling visible in eyes and posture.

## Camera
Use classical silent grammar: locked-off tableaux, slow push-ins on emotion, under-cranked energy for chases, iris closes on endings.

## Lighting
Use high-contrast vintage light: hard key with deep shadows, glowing practicals, moonlit painted-backdrop nights.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Modern dialogue-scene coverage, contemporary dress, neon color chaos, mumbled subtlety that needs words, and documentary handheld shakiness.
