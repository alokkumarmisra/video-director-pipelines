# Music Video Preset

## Purpose
Create electrifying performance visuals cut to rhythm and attitude.

## Visual Style
- Star-presence performance staging: stage, street, neon, desert
- Wardrobe as statement: iconic repeatable artist looks
- Light as instrument: strobes, lasers, silhouette backlights
- Texture of sound made visible: bass haze, beat-synced cuts of light
- Crowd energy: hands, phone lights, jumping silhouettes
- High-gloss finish with punchy contrast

## Storytelling
Perform the song's arc: intimate verse visuals blooming into explosive chorus imagery. Each chorus return goes bigger than the last.

## Characters
Lock the artist look exactly: hairstyle, outfit, jewelry, tattoos. Backup dancers and band stay consistent; the star is unmistakable every frame.

## Camera
Use rhythmic camera work: whip moves on drops, slow orbits on verses, FPV sweeps through crowds, snap zooms on beat hits.

## Lighting
Use concert-grade light design: color-wash scenes per section, silhouette verses against blinding chorus floods, haze to sculpt every beam.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Static tripod-only coverage, flat office lighting, mismatched artist looks between shots, and sleepy pacing during choruses.
