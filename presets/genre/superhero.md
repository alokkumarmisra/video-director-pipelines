# Superhero Preset

## Purpose
Create bold, iconic visuals of heroism with mythic punch.

## Visual Style
- Larger-than-life heroic iconography: capes, emblems, skylines
- Primary-color costumes with sculpted texture detail
- City-scale stakes: crumbling towers, shockwaves, rescues mid-air
- God-ray skies and dramatic cloud breaks behind heroes
- Impact frames: landing craters, energy bursts, debris rings
- Comic-inspired dynamism rendered cinematically

## Storytelling
Heroes choose: the save, the sacrifice, the stand. Stage moral clarity visually — the hero between danger and the innocent, always.

## Characters
Lock the suit design exactly: emblem, color breaks, cape length, mask lines. Civilians stay consistent so rescues read clearly.

## Camera
Use heroic angles: extreme low angles for power, top-downs for vulnerability, slow-motion orbits around decisive moments, vertigo pulls during falls.

## Lighting
Use epic contrast light: storm-lit skies, sunset backlights for hero shots, energy-source glow in night battles.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Mundane realism without stakes, horror nihilism, muted muddy suits, off-model emblems, and irony that undercuts heroism.
