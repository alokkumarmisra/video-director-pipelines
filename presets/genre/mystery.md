# Mystery Preset

## Purpose
Create moody, enigmatic visuals where every frame hides a clue.

## Visual Style
- Muted vintage-tinged palette: sepia shadows, dusty teal, amber lamps
- Rain, fog, and window reflections that obscure as much as reveal
- Detail-rich interiors: books, letters, photographs, locked drawers
- Mirrors, frames-within-frames, and partial views
- Quiet, painterly compositions with deliberate emptiness
- Evidence-like close-ups: footprints, keys, torn paper, fingerprints

## Storytelling
Withhold and hint: show the clue but not its meaning. Let the viewer play detective — a glance held too long, an object slightly out of place.

## Characters
Describe watchful, guarded characters with period-sensible wardrobe. Keep faces consistent; suspicion must be readable on the same faces throughout.

## Camera
Use slow investigative moves: drifting pans across rooms, rack focus from face to object, peering over-the-shoulder framings, locked-off shots that invite scanning.

## Lighting
Use dim lamplight, overcast daylight, and venetian-blind shadows. Faces often half in shadow — nobody is fully revealed.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Bright flat lighting, sci-fi neon, slapstick energy, explicit reveals too early, and cluttered frames where clues get lost.
