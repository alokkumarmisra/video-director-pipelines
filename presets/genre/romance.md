# Romance Preset

## Purpose
Create tender, emotionally glowing visuals of love and connection.

## Visual Style
- Soft warm palette: blush, gold, rose, candlelight amber
- Dreamy gentle glow with blooming highlights
- Intimate details: intertwined hands, shared glances, letters, flowers
- Rain-kissed streets, sunset beaches, cozy candlelit rooms
- Petals, bokeh lights, and drifting fabric in the air
- Clean luminous skin tones

## Storytelling
Slow down for feeling: the almost-touch, the look held a second too long, the small kindness. Let longing and warmth build through accumulation of tender moments.

## Characters
Describe soft, attractive leads with romantic wardrobe. Keep styling consistent — the same couple must be unmistakable in every scene.

## Camera
Use lingering romantic moves: slow push-ins on faces, gentle orbits around embraces, soft-focus transitions, golden silhouette wides.

## Lighting
Use flattering warm light: golden hour, fairy lights, fireplace glow, moonlit blue with warm practicals. Never harsh, never cold-dominant.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Harsh neon, horror shadows, gritty documentary harshness, action chaos, and cold sterile palettes.
