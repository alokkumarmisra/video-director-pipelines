# Sci-Fi Preset

## Purpose
Create visionary futuristic visuals grounded in believable technology.

## Visual Style
- Sleek future design: starships, stations, megacities, labs
- Holograms, HUD overlays, volumetric displays, light interfaces
- Brushed metal, glass, carbon textures with edge lighting
- Deep-space blacks against cyan, magenta, and white tech glow
- Scale awe: tiny figures in colossal engineered spaces
- Clean geometric compositions with leading tech lines

## Storytelling
Show the future through human moments: a pilot's ritual, an engineer's fix, first contact's silence. Technology is the setting — people are the story.

## Characters
Describe crew, androids, and aliens with precise recurring design: uniform piping, visor shapes, cybernetic details must stay fixed.

## Camera
Use smooth precision moves: zero-g drifts, drone-like glides through structures, slow reveals of massive constructs, clinical locked shots for labs.

## Lighting
Use motivated tech light: console glow, hangar floods, planet-shine, sterile lab whites. Color-code factions and systems consistently.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Medieval fantasy props, rustic-only palettes, horror grime without reason, present-day brand clutter, and magic without mechanism.
