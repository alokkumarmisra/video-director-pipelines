# Fairy Tale Preset

## Purpose
Create timeless storybook visuals full of enchantment and innocence.

## Visual Style
- Storybook illustration come to life: cottages, forests, castles
- Soft painterly textures with warm luminous color
- Gentle magical touches: fireflies, talking animals, sparkling dust
- Quaint handcrafted details: thatch, cobblestone, embroidered cloaks
- Pastel dawns, honeyed afternoons, starry velvet nights
- Wholesome beauty with a hint of gentle peril

## Storytelling
Speak in the old rhythms: humble heroes, kindly helpers, trials of heart. Goodness is active — shown through brave kind deeds, not told.

## Characters
Describe princes, seekers, and woodland friends with fixed storybook designs. Villains are spooky but never truly terrifying.

## Camera
Use gentle storybook moves: drifting aerials over forests, slow approaches to cottages, warm close-ups on wonder-filled faces.

## Lighting
Use soft enchanted light: lantern glow, moonbeams through leaves, sunrise after the dark wood. Darkness always yields to dawn.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Graphic horror, cynical grit, modern technology, photorealistic gore, and frightening imagery unsuitable for families.
