# Action Preset

## Purpose
Create explosive, high-energy visuals with visceral impact.

## Visual Style
- Bold saturated contrast with hot highlights
- Dust, sparks, debris, and motion blur that sell velocity
- Massive scale contrasts: heroes against explosions, vehicles, collapsing structures
- Sweat, grit, and texture on characters mid-effort
- Sharp foreground action against streaked backgrounds
- Fire, smoke, and shockwave atmospheres

## Storytelling
Chain cause and effect at speed: punch lands, glass shatters, car swerves. Every beat escalates — bigger, faster, closer.

## Characters
Describe athletic, battle-worn characters with tactical or rugged wardrobe. Keep faces, gear, and injuries consistent as the fight progresses.

## Camera
Use kinetic camera work: low tracking shots, crash zooms, 360-degree orbits around fighters, FPV-style dives through chaos. Motion should feel dangerous and fast.

## Lighting
Use hard punchy light: harsh sun, muzzle-flash pops, explosion orange against teal shadows, stadium floods at night.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Soft romantic glow, static dialogue staging, slow pastoral pacing, cartoon softness, and bloodless weightlessness.
