# Comedy Preset

## Purpose
Create bright, playful visuals with irresistible comic charm.

## Visual Style
- Bright cheerful palette with sunny saturation
- Clean uncluttered sets that stage the gag clearly
- Expressive faces frozen at the peak of reaction
- Visual joke props: oversized items, silly costumes, slapstick tools
- Bouncy, rounded shapes and friendly textures
- Graphic comedy accents: double-takes, spit-takes, pratfall staging

## Storytelling
Setup, escalation, payoff — in that order, every time. Stage the expectation clearly, then break it visually. Reactions sell the joke harder than the gag itself.

## Characters
Describe likable, expressive characters with distinct comic silhouettes. Keep outfits identical so running gags compound.

## Camera
Use punchy comedy timing: static wide for the setup, snap zoom for the reveal, cut to reaction faces. Hold the take one beat longer than feels safe.

## Lighting
Use bright high-key lighting with soft friendly shadows. Everything readable, nothing moody — the joke must never hide in darkness.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Dark horror grading, bleak realism, subtle arthouse ambiguity, gore, and mean-spirited staging.
