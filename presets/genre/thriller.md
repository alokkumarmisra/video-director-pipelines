# Thriller Preset

## Purpose
Create tense, high-stakes visuals that keep viewers on edge.

## Visual Style
- High-contrast urban realism with cool steel tones
- Rain-slick streets, neon reflections, glass and concrete
- Tight compositions that feel watched and claustrophobic
- Surveillance-style textures: monitors, reflections, peepholes
- Sharp detail on objects that matter: phones, files, weapons, clocks
- Night-heavy palette with sodium-orange and cyan accents

## Storytelling
Every scene should raise a question or tighten a timer. Show pursuit, evasion, discovery, and confrontation — always with something at stake in frame.

## Characters
Describe determined, sharp-eyed characters with practical wardrobe. Repeat exact looks so the chase reads across cuts.

## Camera
Use urgent camera language: tracking shots that follow, whip pans between threats, overheads for entrapment, extreme close-ups on decisive details.

## Lighting
Use hard urban night light: headlights, fluorescents, screen glow, red warning lights. Shadows are places where danger hides.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Pastel softness, static stagey framing, fantasy glow, comedy beats, and relaxed pacing that deflates tension.
