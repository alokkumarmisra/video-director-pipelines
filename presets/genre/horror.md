# Horror Preset

## Purpose
Create frightening, dread-filled visuals that unsettle without graphic gore.

## Visual Style
- Desaturated palette with sickly greens, cold blues, deep blacks
- Deep shadows with shapes half-hidden in darkness
- Decaying textures: peeling paint, rust, dust, cobwebs
- Unsettling negative space and off-center compositions
- Fog, flicker, and grain that feel oppressive
- Suggestive rather than explicit horror imagery

## Storytelling
Build dread through anticipation: what is NOT shown matters most. Linger on empty doorways, still corridors, and too-quiet rooms before any reveal.

## Characters
Keep victims relatable and vulnerable; keep threats mostly obscured — silhouette, reflection, partial glimpse. Repeat exact descriptions so dread compounds across scenes.

## Camera
Use slow creeping push-ins, voyeuristic long-lens framing, sudden stillness, and withheld reversals. Never rush the scare.

## Lighting
Use low-key lighting: single practicals, moonlight slits, flashlight beams, candle flicker. Faces half-lit, backgrounds swallowed by dark.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Bright cheerful lighting, comedy staging, explicit gore close-ups, cute stylization, and anything that breaks tension with silliness.
