# Fantasy Preset

## Purpose
Create magical otherworldly visuals steeped in wonder and myth.

## Visual Style
- Enchanted realms: floating islands, crystal forests, ancient citadels
- Magical light sources: runes, wisps, portals, enchanted flora
- Ornate costume detail: cloaks, armor filigree, elven embroidery
- Mythical creatures with believable anatomy and texture
- Mist, god rays, and shimmering particle atmospheres
- Jewel-toned palette with luminous accents

## Storytelling
Treat magic as real and costly: rituals have weight, creatures have habits, prophecies cast shadows. Wonder first, explanation never.

## Characters
Describe heroes, mages, and creatures with exact recurring features — scar patterns, staff design, armor motifs must repeat precisely.

## Camera
Use mythic scale framing: extreme wides for realms, slow rises for revelations, circling moves around duels and rituals.

## Lighting
Use otherworldly motivated light: bioluminescence, spell glow, eclipse light, aurora skies. Magic always has a color and a source.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Modern tech, mundane office realism, sci-fi chrome, contemporary slang staging, and inconsistent creature designs.
