# Adventure Preset

## Purpose
Create sweeping visuals of exploration, discovery, and wonder.

## Visual Style
- Vast epic landscapes: mountains, jungles, deserts, oceans, ancient ruins
- Golden-hour warmth with deep atmospheric perspective
- Tiny explorers against immense environments for scale
- Weather and elements in motion: wind, waves, sand, mist
- Rich earthy palette with jewel-tone accents
- Maps, compasses, torches, and expedition gear as hero props

## Storytelling
Structure every scene as a step into the unknown: the threshold, the marvel, the obstacle, the triumph. Discovery should feel earned.

## Characters
Describe rugged, curious explorers with weathered gear. Keep outfits, packs, and tools identical across the journey.

## Camera
Use grand adventure framing: aerial reveals, crane rises over ridgelines, dolly-throughs of jungle and ruin, silhouettes on horizons.

## Lighting
Use glorious natural light: sunrise summits, sun-shafted canopies, torch-lit caverns, star-dusted night camps.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Cramped interiors only, flat office lighting, modern tech clutter, horror dread palettes, and static lifeless compositions.
