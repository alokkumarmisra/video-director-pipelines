# Fashion Preset

## Purpose
Create elegant style-first visuals with editorial polish.

## Visual Style
- Striking model poses with garment movement: flowing silk, sharp tailoring
- Minimal luxe sets: seamless backdrops, marble, dramatic architecture
- Fabric detail in macro: weave, drape, shimmer, stitching
- High-fashion color discipline: monochrome stories, single bold accents
- Wind, stride, and turn moments that show clothes alive
- Polished beauty looks with consistent makeup

## Storytelling
Style is the plot: the reveal, the turn, the walk toward camera. Each look gets its entrance — fashion film pacing, not catalog rushing.

## Characters
Keep models, hair, and makeup fixed per look. Outfit details — buttons, hems, accessories — must repeat exactly.

## Camera
Use runway-grade moves: slow tracking walks, circling reveals, dramatic low angles, fabric-close macro glides.

## Lighting
Use sculptural editorial light: hard fashion key, dramatic side splits, glowing seamless whites, moody show-space spots.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Sloppy casual framing, dull flat lighting, wrinkled inconsistent garments, busy distracting backgrounds, and off-brief styling.
