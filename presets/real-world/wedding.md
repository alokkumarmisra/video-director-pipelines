# Wedding Preset

## Purpose
Create timeless romantic visuals of wedding celebrations.

## Visual Style
- Dreamy soft-focus romance: glowing highlights, floating petals, veil in wind
- Ceremony details: rings, hands, garlands, mandaps, aisles, vows
- Couple portraits in golden light with elegant attire
- Decor: florals, candles, fairy lights, draped fabrics
- Candid joy: laughter, tears, embraces, dancing elders
- Creamy ivory-gold-blush palette

## Storytelling
Follow the day's emotional arc: anticipation, the first look, vows, celebration. Small rituals carry the biggest feeling.

## Characters
Keep the couple's outfits, jewelry, and styling identical across scenes. Family looks stay fixed for recognizability.

## Camera
Use graceful wedding moves: slow glides down aisles, gentle orbits around embraces, discreet long-lens candid close-ups.

## Lighting
Use flattering romantic light: golden hour portraits, candlelit receptions, soft window-light preparations.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Harsh flash looks, horror shadows, distracting clutter, inconsistent outfits between ceremony and portraits, and rushed chaotic cutting.
