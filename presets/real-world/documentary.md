# Documentary Preset

## Purpose
Create honest, observational visuals with journalistic integrity.

## Visual Style
- Natural un-staged realism: real faces, real places, real work
- Available-light photography with truthful color
- Hands, tools, and process details in tight insert shots
- Lived-in environments with authentic wear and clutter
- Interview-style eye-level framing with soft natural backgrounds
- Archival texture where memory is invoked: grain, stills, slow zooms

## Storytelling
Observe, don't manufacture: follow real routines, capture unguarded moments, let subjects' actions explain their world. Truth first, beauty second.

## Characters
Describe real-seeming people with specific workaday dress. Keep every person identical across scenes — documentaries never recast.

## Camera
Use respectful documentary moves: patient locked shots, gentle handheld following action, slow reveals of place, intimate but never invasive close-ups.

## Lighting
Use only plausible real light: sun, overcast sky, workshop fluorescents, home lamps. Never glamour-light a subject into fiction.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Staged glamour, fantasy glow, action-movie cutting, beautified unreality, and reenactment cheesiness.
