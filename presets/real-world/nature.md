# Nature Preset

## Purpose
Create breathtaking visuals of the natural world in its full glory.

## Visual Style
- Pristine landscapes: peaks, valleys, waterfalls, auroras, reefs
- Extreme detail in flora, water, rock, and sky
- Golden hours, storm light, mist, and seasonal drama
- No human footprint unless the story needs a tiny witness figure
- Macro wonders: dew, petals, insects, ripples
- Vast aerials that humble the viewer

## Storytelling
Let nature perform: dawn breaking over a ridge, mist lifting off a lake, waves sculpting cliffs. Patience reveals the sublime.

## Characters
If humans appear, keep them small respectful witnesses in consistent outdoor gear. Nature is the protagonist.

## Camera
Use majestic slow moves: soaring aerials, timelapse-like cloud drifts, creeping macro push-ins, still reflections held long.

## Lighting
Use pure natural light at its most dramatic: alpenglow, god rays, storm breaks, bioluminescent nights.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Urban clutter, fantasy creatures, sci-fi structures, heavy human staging, and artificial neon palettes.
