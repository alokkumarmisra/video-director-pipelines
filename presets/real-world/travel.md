# Travel Preset

## Purpose
Create irresistible wanderlust visuals of places worth visiting.

## Visual Style
- Iconic destinations shot at their most flattering hour
- Local life: markets, cafes, festivals, artisans, street food
- Postcard compositions with depth: foreground interest, midground life, landmark behind
- Crisp vibrant color with appetizing warmth
- Transport romance: trains, boats, scooters, mountain roads
- Hidden-gem details alongside famous views

## Storytelling
Arrive, taste, wander, marvel: each scene a postcard with a pulse. Show the journey between wonders, not just the wonders.

## Characters
Keep travelers consistent: same backpack, hat, and outfit. Locals stay authentic and dignified, never props.

## Camera
Use gliding travel moves: drone reveals, walking-paced follows, window-frame passing shots, sunrise timelapse energy.

## Lighting
Use travel-brochure light: golden mornings, blue-hour cities, sun-drenched beaches, lantern-lit old towns.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Gloomy grades, empty attraction shots with no life, disrespectful local caricatures, and sterile stock-photo stiffness.
