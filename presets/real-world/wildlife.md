# Wildlife Preset

## Purpose
Create intimate visuals of wild animals behaving naturally.

## Visual Style
- Animals in authentic habitats with true-to-species detail
- Natural camouflage, fur, feather, and scale textures
- Behavior moments: hunts, play, grooming, first steps, migrations
- Long-lens compression with creamy background separation
- Dust, water spray, and breath vapor for atmosphere
- Dawn and dusk activity light

## Storytelling
Follow behavior arcs: the stalk, the chase, the tender maternal moment. Never anthropomorphize — dignity comes from wildness.

## Characters
Keep each animal individual and fixed: scar patterns, size, markings must repeat. Herds stay species-true.

## Camera
Use wildlife-cameraman patience: hidden-position eye-level shots, slow tracking alongside movement, extreme close-ups on eyes, aerials for migrations.

## Lighting
Use low golden light of dawn and dusk; harsh midday avoided. Backlit dust and rim-lit fur are signature looks.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Cute cartoon styling, human clothes on animals, talking-animal staging, wrong-habitat backdrops, and circus-like setups.
