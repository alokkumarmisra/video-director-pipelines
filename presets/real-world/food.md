# Food Preset

## Purpose
Create mouth-watering culinary visuals that make viewers hungry.

## Visual Style
- Hero dishes styled to perfection: steam, gloss, fresh garnish
- Extreme close-ups on texture: melting cheese, crackling crust, dripping sauces
- Warm appetizing palette with rich browns, reds, and fresh greens
- Rustic wood, marble, and linen surfaces
- Cooking action: flames, chopping, kneading, pouring, sizzling
- Hands of the chef as caring co-stars

## Storytelling
From raw ingredients to first bite: show transformation and craft, then the reward. Hunger builds through process.

## Characters
Keep chefs and diners consistent with fixed aprons and styling. Hands stay clean and capable.

## Camera
Use delicious macro moves: slow overhead spins, gliding side tracks across spreads, steam-rise push-ins, satisfying pour shots.

## Lighting
Use warm appetizing light: window-lit freshness, cozy restaurant ambers, flame flicker on night grills.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Cold blue grades, unappetizing mess staging, plastic-looking food, harsh flash photography looks, and cluttered distracting sets.
