# YouTube Shorts Preset

## Purpose
Create punchy vertical-first visuals that hook viewers in the first second.

## Visual Style
- Vertical 9:16 composition with subjects large and centered
- Instant-hook opening frames: bold action or reaction upfront
- High-saturation pop with crushed-contrast punch
- Big readable gestures and expressions for phone screens
- Rapid visual variety: new angle or surprise every beat
- Loop-friendly endings that replay seamlessly

## Storytelling
Zero buildup: start mid-action, escalate fast, end on a rewatchable payoff. Each second must earn the swipe-stay.

## Characters
Keep creators instantly recognizable: signature outfit and hairstyle fixed. Reactions are the currency — keep faces consistent and expressive.

## Camera
Use snappy vertical moves: crash push-ins, whip transitions, spinning reveals, locked selfie framing for direct address.

## Lighting
Use bold phone-friendly light: bright keys, neon accents, dramatic day-for-night pops. Faces always perfectly exposed.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Slow landscape-only wides, tiny unreadable subjects, dull flat light, long dialogue setups, and endings that fizzle.
