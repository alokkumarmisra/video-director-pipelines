# YouTube Kids Preset

## Purpose
Create extra-safe, joyful visuals approved for the youngest YouTube viewers.

## Visual Style
- Ultra-bright friendly animation with rounded everything
- Simple high-contrast compositions readable on small screens
- Gentle educational play: songs, colors, shapes, friendly animals
- Big expressive faces with constant warm smiles
- Slow predictable motion with no startling changes
- COPPA-calm pacing: soothing, never overstimulating

## Storytelling
One tiny lesson or giggle per beat: wave hello, show something wonderful, celebrate, wave goodbye. Familiar loops comfort young viewers.

## Characters
Use fixed wholesome designs with zero scary features. The same friends greet the child in every scene.

## Camera
Use stillness-first framing: locked friendly shots, very slow push-ins, no whip moves, no surprises entering frame fast.

## Lighting
Use bright cheerful daylight with zero darkness. Every corner of frame feels safe.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Anything frightening, loud visual shocks, fast cuts, dark scenes, realistic violence, ads-style pressure, and age-inappropriate themes.
