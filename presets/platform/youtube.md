# YouTube Preset

## Purpose
Create crisp creator-style visuals optimized for YouTube watch time.

## Visual Style
- Clean high-energy creator aesthetic with vivid punchy color
- Talking-head friendly framing with pleasing blurred backgrounds
- Bold readable thumbnail moments: expressive reactions, clear subjects
- B-roll richness: hands-on demos, location variety, satisfying processes
- Modern LED-accented interiors and bright daylight exteriors
- Sharp 4K-style detail with lively contrast

## Storytelling
Hook in three seconds, deliver constantly, tease what's next. Every scene earns the next click — curiosity gaps over slow build.

## Characters
Keep the host's look fixed: same hairstyle, signature outfit details. Guests stay consistent; the channel has a visual brand.

## Camera
Use dynamic creator moves: punch-in emphasis cuts, smooth gimbal walks, overhead demo shots, subtle speed ramps on action.

## Lighting
Use flattering creator light: big soft key on faces, colorful RGB rim accents, bright even illumination for demos.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Dull flat lighting, endless static shots, muddy thumbnails moments, out-of-brand style swings, and slow intros that lose viewers.
