# Commercial Preset

## Purpose
Create sleek persuasive visuals engineered to sell a product or brand.

## Visual Style
- Immaculate product hero shots: floating, rotating, macro detail
- Aspirational lifestyle vignettes around the product
- Brand-color disciplined palette with premium restraint
- Satisfying transformation moments: before/after, pour, unbox, reveal
- Glossy reflective surfaces and pristine styling
- Logo-safe compositions with clean negative space

## Storytelling
Problem, product, transformation — in seconds. Show the desire, deliver the solution, end on the brand promise. Every frame sells.

## Characters
Keep talent polished and consistent: fixed aspirational styling. Hands interacting with product stay manicured and graceful.

## Camera
Use gliding commercial precision: product-table orbits, macro slider moves, seamless lifestyle transitions, confident logo locks.

## Lighting
Use flawless studio light: softbox perfection on product, glowing lifestyle warmth, crisp edge light for premium separation.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Amateur handheld shakiness, dull flat lighting, competitor-like styling, messy unstructured sets, and off-brand color drift.
