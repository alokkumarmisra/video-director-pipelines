# Instagram Reel Preset

## Purpose
Create thumb-stopping vertical 9:16 visuals built for Instagram Reels: instant hook, watchable without sound, and safe zones respected.

## Visual Style
- True vertical 9:16 composition with subjects large and centered
- Bold first-frame hook: expressive face, striking action, or vivid reveal
- High-saturation pop with clean contrast that survives phone screens
- Big readable gestures and emotions — no tiny background details
- Caption-safe framing: keep faces and key action clear of the top and bottom UI zones
- Rapid visual variety: a fresh angle, zoom, or surprise every beat
- Loop-friendly endings that replay seamlessly

## Storytelling
Hook in the first second, escalate without buildup, end on a rewatchable payoff. Design for silent autoplay: the story must read with motion and expression alone.

## Characters
Keep creators instantly recognizable: signature outfit and hairstyle fixed. Reactions are the currency — keep faces consistent and expressive.

## Camera
Use snappy vertical moves: crash push-ins, whip transitions, spinning reveals, locked selfie framing for direct address.

## Lighting
Use bold phone-friendly light: bright keys, neon accents, dramatic day-for-night pops. Faces always perfectly exposed.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Slow landscape-only wides, tiny unreadable subjects, dull flat light, long dialogue setups, text or faces buried in the top/bottom UI safe zones, and endings that fizzle.
