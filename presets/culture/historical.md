# Historical Preset

## Purpose
Create authentic period visuals that feel genuinely lived-in.

## Visual Style
- Period-accurate architecture, dress, tools, and transport
- Worn lived-in textures: mud, timber, iron, handwoven cloth
- Era-correct light sources only: sun, candles, oil lamps, hearths
- Crowds in believable period variety, not uniform extras
- Muted earthy grade with era-appropriate color limits
- Dirt, weather, and labor visible on hands and clothes

## Storytelling
Show the era through daily life under pressure: markets before battle, letters from home, craftsmen at work. History is people, not costumes.

## Characters
Describe historically accurate dress down to fastenings and fabrics. Keep every garment, weapon, and hairstyle consistent and era-true.

## Camera
Use grounded classical framing: eye-level dialogue, wide establishing shots of streets and fields, restrained movement worthy of the period.

## Lighting
Use only period-plausible light: daylight, firelight, candle glow. Interiors are dim and warm; nights are truly dark beyond the hearth.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Modern objects, zippers and plastic, electric light looks, contemporary hairstyles, fantasy magic, and anachronistic architecture.
