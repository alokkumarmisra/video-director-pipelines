# Mythology Preset

## Purpose
Create grand visuals of ancient myths, gods, and legendary deeds.

## Visual Style
- Divine scale: celestial palaces, cosmic oceans, burning battlefields of legend
- Gods and heroes with radiant detail: crowns, divine weapons, sacred mounts
- Supernatural phenomena: eclipses, celestial rivers, world-serpents, flying chariots
- Rich jewel tones with gold and sacred-flame orange
- Classical composition with epic symmetry
- Weather as drama: divine storms, parting clouds, raining flowers

## Storytelling
Tell the old stories with weight: boons and curses have consequences, duty binds even gods. Every miracle should feel earned by devotion or sacrifice.

## Characters
Fix every divine attribute exactly: skin hue, crown design, weapon, mount, garland, and aura color must repeat precisely across scenes.

## Camera
Use awe-struck framing: extreme low angles on the divine, vast wides for cosmic events, slow ascents toward heavens.

## Lighting
Use radiant divine light: halos, sunrise coronas, firelight on armor, moon-silver on night legends.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Modern dress on ancient figures, cartoonish god designs, disrespectful comedy about sacred stories, sci-fi chrome, and inconsistent divine attributes.
