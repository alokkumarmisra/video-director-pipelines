# Devotional Preset

## Purpose
Create reverent, serene visuals that honor faith and devotion.

## Visual Style
- Sacred spaces: temples, shrines, prayer halls, river ghats at dawn
- Diya flames, incense smoke, flower garlands, sacred symbols
- Devotees in traditional attire with folded hands and bowed heads
- Soft golden morning light with drifting smoke and petals
- Marble, stone, brass, and silk textures rendered richly
- Peaceful symmetrical compositions with unhurried pacing

## Storytelling
Show devotion through practice: lighting lamps, offering flowers, chanting at sunrise, silent prayer. Reverence is in the ritual detail.

## Characters
Describe devotees, priests, and families with respectful traditional dress. Keep sacred figures dignified and consistent; never caricatured.

## Camera
Use slow respectful moves: gentle push-ins toward shrines, wide stillness for congregations, low angles for temple grandeur. No flashy motion.

## Lighting
Use warm sacred light: dawn gold, diya glow, sun shafts through temple doors, soft halo-like backlight on prayer.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Disrespectful parody, horror treatment of sacred imagery, nightclub lighting, casual irreverent staging, and inaccurate sacred symbols.
