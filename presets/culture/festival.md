# Festival Preset

## Purpose
Create joyous visuals of celebration, color, and togetherness.

## Visual Style
- Explosions of festive color: powders, lanterns, fireworks, rangoli, streamers
- Crowds laughing, dancing, and feasting together
- Traditional outfits in their brightest celebratory forms
- Night skies lit by lamps and fireworks; days dusted with color
- Sweets, thalis, gifts, and decorated streets as hero details
- High-energy warmth in every frame

## Storytelling
Celebrate through ritual moments: lighting the first lamp, the color throw, the shared meal, the dance circle. Joy is contagious — show it spreading face to face.

## Characters
Describe celebrants in vivid festive dress with consistent colors and accessories. Keep family groups recognizable across scenes.

## Camera
Use exuberant movement: sweeping crane shots over crowds, spinning dance-floor moves, slow-motion color throws, close-ups on laughing faces.

## Lighting
Use festive glow: strings of bulbs, firework bursts, diya clusters, golden daylight for daytime revelry.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Gloomy desaturated grades, solitary bleak moods, horror lighting, empty streets during a festival, and muted corporate palettes.
