# Kids Video Preset

## Purpose
Create safe, cheerful, colorful content for young children.

## Visual Style
- Colorful 3D preschool animation
- Cute rounded characters
- Soft smooth shapes
- Friendly expressive faces
- Warm lighting
- Clean educational backgrounds
- Simple readable compositions
- Wholesome family-friendly atmosphere

## Storytelling
Use simple visual actions that children can understand immediately.

## Characters
Use friendly proportions and consistent character design.

## Camera
Use smooth, gentle camera movement and clear compositions.

## Lighting
Use warm, soft, appealing lighting.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Violence, frightening imagery, disturbing faces, excessive complexity, visual clutter, and inappropriate content.
