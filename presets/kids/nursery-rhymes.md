# Nursery Rhymes Preset

## Purpose
Create gentle, rhythmic sing-along visuals for classic rhymes.

## Visual Style
- Soft pastel storybook animation
- Sleepy-safe rounded characters and animals
- Repetitive visual motifs that match lyrical repetition
- Dreamy clouds, stars, moonlit meadows, cozy cottages
- Large clear focal subjects with minimal background noise
- Bouncy gentle motion synced to an imagined melody

## Storytelling
Mirror the rhyme's structure visually: each repeated line gets a matching repeated visual with a small delightful variation. Predictability comforts toddlers.

## Characters
Use ultra-consistent sweet designs: same bonnet, same sheep, same spider, every verse. Toddlers notice changes adults miss.

## Camera
Use rocking-gentle moves: slow side-to-side sways, soft push-ins on the singing moment, static holds during refrains.

## Lighting
Use warm nursery light: nightlight glow, moonbeam softness, sunny morning warmth. Never dark, never harsh.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Loud jarring visuals, scary faces, fast cuts, complex detailed backgrounds, and anything unsuitable before bedtime.
