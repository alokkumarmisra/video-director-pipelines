# Silent Kids Preset

## Purpose
Create wordless visual stories young children follow with pure delight.

## Visual Style
- Pantomime-clear 3D animation with big readable emotions
- Simple cause-and-effect gags: ball rolls, chase begins, hug ends
- Bright friendly worlds with zero text or dialogue dependence
- Expressive body language: pointing, peeking, tiptoeing, celebrating
- Musical-movement feel: actions land on invisible beats
- Universal humor: silliness any child on earth understands

## Storytelling
No words means crystal clarity: want, obstacle, funny attempt, happy resolution. Every beat must work with the sound off.

## Characters
Use instantly readable designs with huge expressive eyes. Keep outfits fixed; personality comes from movement, not costume changes.

## Camera
Use patient observational framing: wide enough to catch the gag setup, push-ins on reaction faces, no fast cutting that loses toddlers.

## Lighting
Use warm bright daylight throughout. Nothing hides in shadow when there are no words to explain it.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Dialogue-dependent plots, on-screen text, scary surprises, complex multi-thread stories, and frantic editing.
