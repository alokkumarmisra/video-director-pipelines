# Animals Learning Preset

## Purpose
Create lovable animal visuals that teach names, looks, and sounds.

## Visual Style
- Cute stylized animals with accurate identifying features
- Each animal in its simple natural setting: duck-pond, lion-savanna
- Big clear animal hero shots with name-friendly staging
- Soft rounded 3D or storybook 2D style, consistently one of them
- Gentle habitat details: grass, water ripples, trees, burrows
- Sound-moment staging: open beaks, mooing mouths, roaring poses

## Storytelling
Meet one animal per beat: arrival, signature trait or sound moment, happy goodbye wave. Familiar structure builds vocabulary fast.

## Characters
Keep each species design fixed: same spots, same mane, same colors. A returning animal must be instantly recognizable.

## Camera
Use friendly wildlife framing: eye-level with the animal, slow push-ins on faces, wide habitat reveals for context.

## Lighting
Use sunny cheerful daylight with soft shadows. Animals always well-lit and welcoming.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Predator-prey violence, scary realistic teeth close-ups, wrong habitats, inconsistent species design, and frightening sounds staging.
