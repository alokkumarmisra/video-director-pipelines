# Cinematic Preset

## Purpose
Create polished film-look visuals with a professional cinematic feel.

## Visual Style
- Rich filmic color grade with balanced contrast
- Shallow depth of field where it serves the story
- Detailed, production-designed environments
- Naturalistic textures on skin, fabric, and surfaces
- Clean compositions with clear subject separation
- Subtle film grain for an organic feel
- Widescreen, composed framing

## Storytelling
Let each shot play like a film still: one clear dramatic idea per scene, with visual subtext supporting the emotion.

## Characters
Keep characters consistent in face, wardrobe, age, and build across every scene. Describe the same identifying features each time.

## Camera
Use deliberate cinematic camera language: slow push-ins for intimacy, wide establishing shots for scale, smooth lateral moves for transitions. No erratic or shaky motion unless the scene demands it.

## Lighting
Use motivated, dramatic lighting: soft key light on faces, practical lights in frame, gentle rim light for separation. Favor golden hour, moody interiors, and motivated night lighting.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Flat lighting, over saturated cartoon colors, distorted faces, extra limbs, watermark-like artifacts, and abrupt style shifts between scenes.
