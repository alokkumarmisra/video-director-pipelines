# Photorealistic Preset

## Purpose
Create lifelike, photographic visuals indistinguishable from real camera footage.

## Visual Style
- True-to-life proportions and anatomy
- Realistic skin texture with pores and subtle imperfections
- Physically accurate materials: metal, glass, fabric, water
- Natural color rendition, no stylization
- Real-world clutter and detail at believable levels
- 35mm-photography sharpness with natural depth of field

## Storytelling
Ground every scene in observable reality. Actions and reactions should feel captured, not staged — small authentic gestures over theatrical poses.

## Characters
Describe realistic humans with specific age, ethnicity, clothing brands/styles, and natural expressions. Repeat the exact description in every scene.

## Camera
Use realistic camera behavior: eye-level or motivated angles, natural handheld micro-movement only when appropriate, documentary-style framing for candid moments.

## Lighting
Use physically plausible light: sun, window light, street lamps, practicals. Match direction, color temperature, and shadow softness to the stated time and place.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Painterly or illustrated looks, plastic skin, glowing eyes, impossible physics, floating objects, warped architecture, and text artifacts.
