# Anime Preset

## Purpose
Create expressive Japanese anime visuals with dynamic cinematic energy.

## Visual Style
- Clean anime linework with cel shading and gradient highlights
- Detailed expressive eyes with catchlights
- Stylized hair with distinct color blocks and shine bands
- Lush painterly backgrounds with atmospheric perspective
- Speed lines, sakuga-style motion accents, and impact frames for action
- Balanced mix of detailed key shots and clean dialogue framing

## Storytelling
Stage emotion through anime grammar: held reaction shots, symbolic cutaways (petals, wind, light), and escalating shot intensity toward climaxes.

## Characters
Keep anime model sheets fixed: same face structure, eye color, hairstyle, uniform/outfit, and accessory details in every scene.

## Camera
Use dynamic anime cinematography: dramatic low angles, dutch tilts for unease, fast zoom punches for reveals, slow orbiting shots for emotional peaks.

## Lighting
Use expressive anime lighting: rim-lit hair, glowing skies, dramatic sunset backlights, and stylized shadow shapes that follow the mood.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Western cartoon proportions, photorealistic faces, 3D-render stiffness, inconsistent eye styles, and garbled background text.
