# Cartoon Preset

## Purpose
Create bold, fun 2D cartoon visuals with strong personality.

## Visual Style
- Flat cel-shaded 2D cartoon rendering
- Thick clean outlines and graphic shapes
- Bright punchy color blocks with minimal gradients
- Exaggerated expressive faces and squash-and-stretch poses
- Simple stylized backgrounds that keep focus on characters
- Playful graphic details: motion lines, impact stars, expressive symbols

## Storytelling
Go big and legible: exaggerated reactions, visual gags, and poses readable at a glance. Comedy and charm come from expressive over-acting.

## Characters
Lock the model sheet: same head shape, eye style, outfit, and color palette in every scene. Exaggeration must stay on-model.

## Camera
Use snappy cartoon staging: flat front-on shots for gags, dramatic low angles for reveals, quick push-ins for punchlines. Keep compositions simple and centered on the action.

## Lighting
Use bright even cartoon lighting with simple graphic shadows. Mood comes from background color, not complex light setups.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Realistic textures, photographic detail, horror realism, muddy palettes, and off-model character drift.
