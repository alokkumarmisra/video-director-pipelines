# Portrait Preset

## Purpose
Create intimate, character-focused visuals where faces and emotion carry the story.

## Visual Style
- Close, personal framing with creamy background separation
- Flattering shallow depth of field
- Refined skin rendering with gentle detail
- Wardrobe and styling chosen to reveal personality
- Minimal distracting background elements
- Quiet, gallery-like composition discipline

## Storytelling
Tell the story through micro-expression: a glance, a half-smile, a held gaze. Let stillness and subtle movement do the work of pages of dialogue.

## Characters
Describe the subject's face precisely — age, features, expression baseline, hairstyle, wardrobe — and repeat it exactly so the same person appears in every scene.

## Camera
Stay close: close-ups, medium close-ups, and slow push-ins. Camera moves are barely perceptible drifts that keep the eyes locked on the subject.

## Lighting
Use flattering portrait light: large soft key, gentle fill, subtle rim for hair separation. Window light and soft studio setups dominate.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Wide empty framings, harsh overhead light, distorted facial features, busy backgrounds that steal focus, and rapid cutting energy.
