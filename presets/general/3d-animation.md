# 3D Animation Preset

## Purpose
Create high-quality stylized 3D animated visuals with broad family appeal.

## Visual Style
- Smooth stylized 3D rendering with soft subsurface scattering
- Appealing rounded proportions and expressive oversized eyes
- Rich saturated color palettes with harmonious grading
- Clean readable silhouettes for every character and prop
- Soft global illumination with gentle ambient occlusion
- Detailed but uncluttered environments

## Storytelling
Play actions broad and readable: clear poses, anticipation, and follow-through so every beat lands visually even without dialogue.

## Characters
Use consistent stylized proportions, hairstyle, outfit colors, and facial design in every scene. Keep the same character rig feel throughout.

## Camera
Use smooth animated camera moves: gentle arcs, push-ins on emotion, wide shots for comedy staging. Keep motion fluid and eased, never jittery.

## Lighting
Use cheerful three-point lighting with warm keys and colorful rim/fill accents. Interiors feel cozy, exteriors feel bright and inviting.

## Continuity
Maintain consistent characters, clothing, props, environments, colors, and visual style between scenes.

## Avoid
Photorealistic gore, horror lighting, distorted anatomy, uncanny hyper-detail on faces, and live-action texture mixing.
